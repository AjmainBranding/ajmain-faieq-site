The problem

You know your subject. The video doesn’t show it.

You help people every day in private. On camera the same ideas land slower, and viewers click away before the part that would have earned their trust.

  • 0:00The opening gives no reason to stay. Viewers decide in the first 30 seconds, before you have said anything of value.
  • 2:00Long stretches of talking with nothing to look at. A good point is hard to follow when it is only said.
  • 6:00Claims with no proof on screen. People who have been burned before don’t take your word for it.
If the idea is valuable, people will watch anyway.

They won’t. Viewers stay when the video keeps them watching long enough to take the idea in. That is an editing problem, and it is the one I fix.

The method

Three things I do to every video.

I don’t add effects to look busy. Each edit is there so the viewer understands faster and has a reason to keep watching.

First 30 seconds

Preview the payoff

The opening shows a glimpse of what is coming later, so viewers know what they get by staying.

Every line

Match it to a visual

When you make a point, the screen shows what the viewer needs to see to follow it. Questions get answered before they turn into confusion.

Every claim

Show the proof with it

When you state a result, the matching screenshot or source appears at that moment. Nothing is left for the viewer to take on trust.

Questions

What people ask before applying.

“Will the video still sound like me?”

Yes. I correct the outcome, not the creator. Your words, your pace and your opinions stay. What changes is how easy you are to follow and how long people stay.

“I already have an editor. Why switch?”

If your videos already hold viewers to the end, don’t. If people leave early or the videos bring views but no enquiries, the edit is usually where it goes wrong. Send me a recent video and I will show you what I would change.

“How much of my time does this take?”

One call at the start. After that, a couple of minutes per video to send the footage with a line on what it is for. Everything else is on me.

“What does it cost?”

Projects start at $2,000 a month for four videos. The price depends on how long your videos are and how much on-screen design each one needs. You get an exact price in writing before we start, and it does not change mid-project.

“What if the edit doesn’t improve my retention?”

Then your fourth edit is free. If my first three edits don’t keep more of your viewers past the first 30 seconds than your last three comparable videos did, on average, you don’t pay for the fourth. I need your retention numbers before we start, and each video has to stay public for 14 days so there is something to measure.

“My channel is small. Is this still for me?”

Channel size matters less than the offer behind it. One of the consultants I edit for had under 1,000 subscribers when we started. If one new client covers the cost, the numbers work.

“Do you edit sales videos as well?”

Yes. I edit VSLs for coaching programs and paid communities, using the same method: every claim gets its proof on screen.
